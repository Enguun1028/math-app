# ЭЕШ / ЭШ mathematics: what the exam tests (research 2026-10-14)

Stream: entrance exam (Элсэлтийн ерөнхий шалгалт, ЭЕШ; called Элсэлтийн шалгалт, ЭШ in the 2025–2026 papers and news). Run by Боловсролын үнэлгээний төв (БҮТ, eec.mn).
Companion data: `esh-topic-weights.json` (155 items from 5 official papers mapped to `concepts.txt` ids).
Everything below has a source. My own judgement (topic mapping, item descriptions, app advice) is marked **(draft)**.

---

## 1. Exam format

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Time | 100 minutes for mathematics (also physics and chemistry; language and other subjects get 80) | 2022 журам, item 3.9 [S3]; 2021 blueprint [S1]; 2017 blueprint [S2] |
| Part 1 (Нэгдүгээр хэсэг, СОНГОХ ДААЛГАВАР) | 36 multiple-choice items, 5 options (A–E), 72 points in total | papers 2022 A, 2023 B, 2025 A [S4–S6]; 2021 blueprint [S1] |
| Points per item in part 1 | Items 1–8: 1 point; 9–28: 2 points; 29–36: 3 points (8 + 40 + 24 = 72). This is printed in the 2025 paper. The item bank shows the same split for 2022 and 2023; 2021 used 10×1 + 16×2 + 10×3 | 2025 A paper [S6]; item bank [S7] |
| Part 2 (Хоёрдугаар хэсэг, НӨХӨХ ДААЛГАВАР) | 4 multi-step tasks, numbered 2.1–2.4, 7 points each (28 in total). The answer is not chosen from options. Students fill digit boxes (a, b, c, …) on the answer sheet. Each task has 3–4 sub-steps that walk through one solution, and the 2025 paper shows points per sub-step (for example 3 + 2 + 2) | papers [S4–S6] |
| Total | 100 raw points (анхны оноо), reported as a scaled score (хэмжээст оноо) on 200–800 | 2025 stats article [S9]; 2021 blueprint [S1] |
| Calculator | **Not allowed.** The rules ban phones, calculators (тооны машин), tables, formula summaries, books, notebooks and other materials. Only a soft pencil is used | 2022 журам, item 3.8 [S3] |
| Formula sheet | The 2025 paper prints a short "useful formulas" box on page 1: vertex form of a parabola, cone volume, mean and variance of a discrete random variable, dot product. It also prints a blank coordinate grid | 2025 A paper p.1 [S6] |
| Variants | 4 variants per year (A, B, C, D) | item bank [S7]; eec.mn uploads [S4–S6] |
| Marking and results | Answer sheets are machine-read. The raw score is shown right after the exam; the scaled score is published within 24 hours. A certificate is valid for 2.5 years | 2022 журам, items 4.1–4.6 [S3] |
| Raw-to-800 conversion | **Not published** in any source I could open. In the news the mean scaled score is always 500 (2024: mean 500, median 496), so the scale looks normed to mean 500, but the formula is not public | [S8], [S9] |
| University threshold | 490 scaled points in 2025. In maths, 48% (13,887) of candidates were below 490 in 2025, compared with 46% in 2024 | ikon 2025 [S10] |

### Official blueprint (Ерөнхий блюпринт), 2021, signed by the БҮТ director [S1]

This is the latest official blueprint I found (40 tasks = 36 part-1 items + 4 part-2 tasks).

| Strand (Үндсэн агуулга) | Remember | Understand | Apply | Analyse | Tasks | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Тоо тоолол (number) | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 6 | 15.0% |
| Алгебр | 2 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 14 | 35.0% |
| Анализын эхлэл (calculus) | 1 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 5 | 12.5% |
| Геометр ба тригонометр | 2 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 10 | 25.0% |
| Магадлал, статистик | 1 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 5 | 12.5% |
| Total | 8 (20%) | 12 (30%) | 12 (30%) | 8 (20%) | 40 | 100% |

The blueprint also lists sub-topics (дэд агуулга) with the grades they come from (Roman numerals = grade):
- **Тоо тоолол:** number sets and operations (VIII–X), powers (X), standard form (IX–X), comparing and rounding (VIII–IX), percent, ratio and proportion (VIII–IX)
- **Алгебр:** algebraic expressions and formulas (IX–X), functions and graphs (X–XI), matrices (X), equations and inequalities (IX–XII), systems (XI), polynomials (XII), sets (X), sequences (XI)
- **Анализын эхлэл:** derivative (XI), integral (XI)
- **Геометр ба тригонометр:** circles and polygons (X), coordinate geometry (X–XI), plane vectors (X), space vectors (XI), trigonometry (X–XI), geometric transformations (IX–X), measurement (IX–X)
- **Магадлал, статистик:** data analysis (X–XI), combinatorics (VI–VII), probability (IX–X)

The note on the blueprint says the content is the compulsory content of the basic and upper-secondary curriculum. So **the exam covers grades 6–12, with most weight on grades 9–12**.

The older 2017 blueprint [S2] had only 4 strands: Тоо тоолол 10 (25%), Алгебр 18 (45%), Геометр 7 (17.5%), Магадлал статистик 5 (12.5%). Calculus was a separate strand only from the 2021 version.

The public item bank (eyeshitems.eec.mn) uses the same sub-topic names for its filter [S7]. Its per-topic counts are the same for every year, so they look like a 4-variant template rather than real counts. Per variant that gives: equations/inequalities 3, functions and graphs 3, derivative 3, and 2 each for expressions, sequences, polynomials, number expressions, systems, integral, vector, transformations, coordinate geometry, trigonometry and data. Matrix, sets, circles and polygons, combinatorics and probability get 1 each. These per-variant numbers are my reading of the filter **(draft)**.

### Cognitive level per year (part 1, variant A, official item-bank tags [S7])

| Year | Remember | Understand | Apply | Analyse |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2018 | 8 | 4 | 11 | 12 |
| 2019 | 8 | 4 | 12 | 12 |
| 2020 | 8 | 4 | 12 | 12 |
| 2021 | 10 | 10 | 12 | 4 |
| 2022 | 8 | 12 | 12 | 4 |
| 2023 | 8 | 11 | 13 | 4 |

After 2020 the exam shifted from "analyse" items to "understand/apply" items.

### Coming changes (not final)
- Feb 2024, БҮТ: from 2026 candidates would choose level I/II subject tests. Maths would have two levels: level II for maths-heavy majors and level I for majors that only use maths [S11].
- Jun 2026, Ministry (L. Ganbat): changes will be piloted next year and a new format will start from **2028**. It would have a compulsory "learning-ability test" (Суралцах чадварын шалгалт) with joint Mongolian + maths content, and then Математик-1 (advanced, for engineering and maths) and Математик-2 (required level, for teaching, chemistry, biology). The stated aim is an SAT-like format [S12].
- The two articles disagree on timing. The 2026 exam itself (25–28 June 2026) still used the usual ЭШ subjects [S13]. **The app's ЭЕШ goal should follow the current 36 + 4 format, and the content should be revisited in 2027.**

## 2. Topic blueprint: observed in real papers (my mapping, draft)

**Papers used** (all official, from БҮТ):
- 2021 variant A, part 1 (item bank)
- 2022 variant A, both parts (item bank + PDF)
- 2023 variant A, part 1 (item bank)
- 2023 variant B, part 2 (PDF)
- 2025 variant A, both parts (PDF)

That is 156 items. One 2021 item could not be identified because the bank shows an unrelated picture, so 155 are mapped. I counted each item once under its main `concepts.txt` id. Full list with one-line descriptions: `esh-topic-weights.json` → `weights.*.examples`.

### By exam strand (observed vs official 2021 blueprint)

| Strand | Items | Share of items | Share of points | Blueprint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Тоо тоолол | 16 | 10.3% | 6.7% | 15% |
| Алгебр | 48 | 31.0% | 30.2% | 35% |
| Анализын эхлэл | 24 | 15.5% | 17.8% | 12.5% |
| Геометр ба тригонометр | 43 | 27.7% | 28.6% | 25% |
| Магадлал, статистик | 24 | 15.5% | 16.7% | 12.5% |

Number skills get fewer items than the blueprint suggests, and those items are the cheap 1-point ones. Calculus and probability get a little more, and they appear in the 7-point part 2 tasks.

### By the app's grade for each concept (points share)
Grade 5: 7% · 6: 4.6% · 7: 4.6% · **8: 25%** · 9: 11% · **10: 13%** · **11: 21%** · **12: 13.5%**

### Most frequent concepts (count over 155 items, points in brackets)

| Concept | Items | Points | Typical item |
|---|---|---|---|
| def-int | 7 | 26 | definite integral; area between curves; part-2 area/volume task |
| poly-div | 6 | 14 | remainder theorem, factor with a known root |
| vector | 6 | 13 | express a vector through a ratio point |
| quad-fn | 5 | **25** | part 2.1 every year: a quadratic with parameters (vertex, min/max on an interval, |f(x)|=k) |
| rand-var | 5 | 14 | distribution table, expected value, variance |
| sets | 5 | 7 | union/intersection counts, Venn diagram |
| spread | 5 | 10 | quartiles, IQR, variance |
| trig-fn | 5 | 9 | identities, reducing angles, counting solutions |
| deriv-app | 4 | 15 | monotonic intervals, extremum, optimisation (part 2) |
| abs, alg-frac, complex, coord-geo, tangent-slope | 4 each | 7–9 | |
| similar | 3 | 13 | similar triangles in part 2 geometry |
| tree | 2 | 14 | part-2 multi-step probability (die walk, card game) |

**Part 2 pattern (3 papers):**
- **2.1** is always a quadratic function with a parameter (quad-fn).
- One task is calculus: maximise with the derivative, or area/volume with an integral.
- One task is geometry: a solid (pyramid, cones) or a parallelogram with similar triangles.
- One task is multi-step probability.

These 4 tasks are worth 28% of the score. **(draft observation, 3 papers only)**

Concepts that were never the *main* topic of an item: early-grade arithmetic (frac, dec, pct, int, gcd), construct, proof, congruent, parallel, bearing, scatter, induction, normal, rat-fn, quad-eq, pyth. Several of these (pyth, quad-eq, factor, fractions, percent) are still needed as steps inside other items. The full list is `never_main_concept_in_sample` in the JSON.

### Example of the level (my own paraphrase, no copied items)
- **1-point:** value of sin 1140°; upper quartile of 10 numbers; median of 9 numbers; size of a matrix product.
- **2-point:** remainder of a polynomial; normal line to y = 1/(x−1); dot-product parameter; variance of a binomial count.
- **3-point:** coefficient in a trinomial expansion; Newton cooling differential equation; maximum horizontal distance between a line and an exponential curve.

## 5. What this means for the app (draft)

1. **ЭЕШ goal, which topics first:** quad-fn, def-int/integral, deriv/deriv-app/tangent-slope, poly-div, vector, rand-var/spread, trig-fn, complex, abs, alg-frac, coord-geo, sets. Together they make up about half (49.5%) of the points in the sample.
2. **Practice in the exam's own format:**
   - Part 1: 5-option items, no calculator.
   - Part 2: a guided multi-step task where the student fills digit boxes. This fits a "step dots" walkthrough well.
3. **Show the formula box** the exam provides (parabola vertex form, cone volume, E(X)/Var(X), dot product). Students do not need to memorise these, but they need to know when to use them.
4. **Grade 8 concepts carry about 25% of the points** (quad-fn, alg-frac, similar, coord-geo, chord-tangent), so lower-grade students can work towards the exam early.
