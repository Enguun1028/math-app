## A. Verified with source

1. PISA 2022 (Mongolia's first): math mean 425 (OECD 472, Asian average 451), 47th of 81. Ministry: 2.3 years behind the international average, 1.2 behind Asia. 49% at Level 2+ (OECD 69%); 2% top performers. Reading 378 (36% Level 2+), science 412. Sample 6,999 students in 195 schools; 86% of 15-year-olds in grade 10. [high]
https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2023/11/pisa-2022-results-volume-i-and-ii-country-notes_2fca04b9/mongolia_a2a3f956/a93e1396-en.pdf , https://news.mn/en/800233/ , https://moe.gov.mn/post/134474

2. PISA details [high, OECD factsheet]:
- Wealth gap: top vs bottom socio-economic quartile 94 points; SES explains 18% of variance (OECD 15%).
- Gender: girls +6 points; low performers 53% of boys, 49% of girls.
- 59% say the math teacher gives extra help when needed (OECD 70%); 33% distracted by digital devices.
- 61% had the school building closed over 3 months. During remote learning, 47% had trouble understanding assignments at least weekly, 40% had trouble finding someone to help (OECD 34% and 24%). 64% confident they can motivate themselves.
- 38% in schools where lack of teaching staff hinders instruction; 38% where inadequate or poorly qualified staff does.
- 22% do not feel safe on the way to school (OECD 8%).
- Ministry: weakest at applying math to solve problems and at reasoning ("математикийн мэдлэгээ хэрэглэх, учир шалтгааныг илрүүлэх чадвар сул"); PISA items were completely new compared with familiar exam formats (moe.gov.mn/post/134474).

3. Math entrance exam raw scores (out of 100), national mean: 2020 27, 2021 26, 2022 26, 2023 27, 2024 24, 2025 19. 2025: 28,637 takers, median 18, 1% above 60. 2024: 23,002 takers, median 21, 2% above 60. Scaled score normed to mean 500, hiding the decline. ikon.mn argues the 2024 threshold (480) was reachable by guessing. By aimag 2024: 21 (Bulgan) to 28 (Sükhbaatar); Ulaanbaatar 23 (11,577 takers). [high for numbers, medium for "test too hard"]
https://ikon.mn/n/3fq9 , https://ikon.mn/n/36qp

4. Exam restructured from 2026: mandatory "Суралцах чадварын шалгалт" (Mongolian language and core math, threshold 400). Subject tests Математик I (average) and Математик II (advanced). Official name: Элсэлтийн шалгалт (ЭШ). [high]
https://ikon.mn/n/32ud

5. Weak foundations before COVID: 41.2% of grade 5 proficient in math (Education Quality Assessment 2018, via World Bank). Soum schools lag aimag centres, which lag Ulaanbaatar. EGMA 2017: Kazakh-minority pupils 10% lower. Grade 2 addition fluency flat at about 14 per minute 2017-2023 (snippet only). [high; fluency medium]
https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/949791604487617663/pdf/Towards-Mongolia-s-Long-Term-Development-Policy-Vision-2050-Advancing-Education-Equity-Efficiency-and-Outcomes.pdf , https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099145012062235079/pdf/P14811003c30f90f60a4c6049368b9fdec6.pdf

6. COVID: closed from January 2020, briefly reopened autumn 2020, fully reopened 1 September 2021; теле хичээл with no interaction; ger-district interruptions. Ministry: about 50% of instruction over two school years was remote/TV; 174,000 children in actual learning loss; 2021-2023 recovery plan. Sept-Oct 2021 diagnostic of 297,000 students grades 2-12: grade 2 highest (63.4%), grade 9 lowest (34.2%), no math-specific figure. UNICEF: primary numeracy proficiency 53.5% (2019) to 38.2% (2021). [high]
https://kuwaittimes.com/mongolian-pupils-go-back-to-school-and-end-long-education-exile/ , https://ikon.mn/n/27rn , https://moe.gov.mn/post/114456 , https://www.unicef.org/mongolia/education

7. Structure and curriculum: 10 to 11 years in 2004, 12 years from 2008/09 (entry age 6); 6 primary + 3 lower secondary + 3 upper secondary (2023 Education Law). World Bank: frequent changes in syllabus, curriculum and textbooks detrimental to quality; primary and lower-secondary hours 844 above OECD; teachers report too much content. 12,000+ teacher comments on 118 textbooks: hard to teach, overlapping, unevenly distributed, objectives too broad. Only 16% of expected textbooks purchased in 2018. ADB: rote memorisation, dense textbooks; secondary curriculum reform planned 2025-2028. [high that reported; WB paper from 2020]
https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/linked-documents/57176-001-ssa.pdf

8. Overcrowding (ADB, school year 2022): Ulaanbaatar 363,377 students, up about 100,000 since 2018/19; average class 33.5 (national 30.2). 6 three-shift schools nationally; WB: 88 of 811 assessed schools ran more than two shifts. June 2023 class cap 45. 53,000+ new teachers needed by 2030; many schools lack subject specialists. [high]

9. Herder children and minorities: 34,700 children in 522 dormitories; 71.3% of dormitories use carried or well water, 61% outdoor latrines. Dropout high in Zavkhan, Uvs, Bayan-Ölgii. Kazakh and Tuva minorities underperform; 23 textbooks translated into Kazakh (grades 1-5). 13.4% of boys vs 5.1% of girls not in upper secondary. [high]
https://www.unicef.org/mongolia/education

10. Tutoring (OSI 2005-06 survey of first-year university students): 71% tutored in final year; 54.2% in mathematics (largest subject); about 40% by their own class teachers; 63.7% believe teachers favour tutored students; pressure from teachers reported. Math tutoring helped "better learn topics taught in school" (73%) and "systematize topics learned earlier" (69%). iMath online annual class to the 2026 ЭШ: 1,000,000₮. [high but dated; prices low-medium]

## Implications (agent's list)

- Diagnostic, not a grade label; trace back to primary arithmetic, fractions, proportion. COVID cohorts (grades 1-9 in 2020-21) are now teenagers.
- Use "хоцрогдол нөхөх" / "түвшин нөхөх" wording.
- Application and reasoning first: contextualised problems (prices, distance, livestock) alongside drills.
- Replace the missing helper: hints and worked examples.
- Low bandwidth and offline; short, resumable.
- Free or cheap alternative to 1M₮ tutoring.
- Later ЭШ prep aligned to the 2026 format (aptitude core math, Math I); move students from about 15-20 raw to above threshold.
- Kazakh-language support later.
