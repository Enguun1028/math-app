About half of 15-year-olds are below basic math level

PISA 2022, Mongolia's first international test of 15-year-olds: 49% reached Level 2 (basic level) against 69% on average across the OECD, a group of 38 mostly high-income countries. Mean score 425 against 472.

Монгол · the context

How big the gap is in Mongolia

The gap starts in primary school, COVID widened it, and the entrance exam has kept falling since.

Students at or above basic math level (PISA 2022)
PISA is the OECD's international test of 15-year-olds. "OECD average" means the average of its 38 member countries, mostly high-income systems such as Japan, Korea, Germany and the US.
Mean score: Mongolia 425, OECD 472, Asia 451. 2% top performers (OECD 9%). The top wealth quarter scored 94 points above the bottom quarter. OECD
Primary-school numeracy proficiency, before and after COVID
Ministry diagnostic test. Before COVID, only 41.2% of grade 5 students were proficient in math (2018). UNICEF · World Bank
No one to ask

During remote learning, 40% had nobody to help them with schoolwork (OECD 24%), and 47% had trouble understanding assignments every week. These are PISA 2022 student-survey answers about the school closures. OECD

High
Overloaded curriculum

Frequent syllabus and textbook changes. 844 more instruction hours than the OECD in primary and lower secondary. Teachers say "too much content", and teaching leans on memorization. World Bank · ADB

High
Weak at applying

The ministry's own reading of PISA: students are weakest at applying math and reasoning. PISA-style problems felt completely new next to familiar exam formats. Ministry of Education

High
Crowded schools

Ulaanbaatar gained about 100,000 students since 2018. The class cap is now 45. 53,000+ new teachers are needed by 2030. Only 59% say their math teacher gives extra help (OECD 70%). ADB · OECD

High
Rural and minority

34,700 children live in 522 dormitories (дотуур байр). Soum schools lag aimag centers, which lag the capital. Kazakh-minority pupils scored 10% lower in early math. World Bank

High
Tutoring economy

In the last rigorous survey (2005-06), 54% were tutored in math, often by their own teacher. A year of online exam prep costs about 1,000,000₮ today. Open Society survey · iMath

Later topics (logarithms, the unit circle, limits, derivatives) mostly fail because of these seven. A student stuck on a derivative should be routed down the map, not given more at the top.

Fractions
1/3 + 1/4 = 2/7

Adding tops and bottoms. Asked to estimate 12/13 + 7/8, only 27% of US 8th graders chose 2.

Fractions
1/8 > 1/6

"Because 8 is bigger than 6." Whole-number rules applied to fractions.

Decimals
0.125 > 0.3

"Longer is bigger." About 40% of grade 5 students do this.

Negatives
−3 − 5 = 8

"Two negatives make a positive", applied everywhere.

Equals sign
8 + 4 = 12 + 5

Filling in the answer to the left side, not balancing both sides.

Variables
2x + 3 = 5x

Joining unlike terms because they sit next to each other.

Each classic mistake is a ready-made diagnostic question: the wrong answer a student picks tells us which earlier sphere to send them to.

The hidden gap: math words

Math vocabulary correlates with math performance at r ≈ 0.49, and the link is stronger at secondary level. This matches your primary user: teens who lack specific terms.

The teenager

What keeps a student stuck

The gap is only half of it. The other half is how it feels to be behind at 15.

Anxiety and avoidance feed each other

High

65% of 15-year-olds worry about poor math marks. Anxious students pick easy, low-reward problems to avoid effort.

Make the first step tiny.

Shame blocks asking

Med-high

Low achievers avoid asking for help most, because needing help feels like proof they can't do it.

A private phone app removes the audience.

Competence is the anxiety cure

Medium

Across 50 studies, building skill cut anxiety and raised scores together, most of all for students over 12.

Show real progress, not pep talks.

Slogans barely work

Medium

Growth-mindset messaging alone has a tiny effect on achievement (d = 0.08).

Skip the motivational quotes.

Relevance helps the least confident

Med-high

Connecting a topic to one's own life raised interest and grades, mainly for students who expected to fail.

One real-life line per topic.

Grade labels sting

Medium

"You are at grade 4 level" stigmatizes. Framing review as unlocking a current goal does not.

No grade labels on foundations.
What works

Catch-up methods with evidence

Effect sizes in standard deviations from randomized trials and meta-analyses. Studies measure differently, so read the bars as rough strength, not an exact ranking.

Effect on math achievement
The blue bar is Mindspark, the closest analogue to our app: adaptive software for grades 6-9 in India, most students several grades behind. Separately, interleaved review scored 61% vs 38% on a delayed test (d = 0.83), measured on a different kind of test so not shown on this scale.

The 5% problem

Khan Academy, IXL and similar apps work at the recommended dose, but only about 5-9% of students reach it, mostly the ones who would succeed anyway. Education Next · Khan Academy

Medium-high

Content alone didn't work

In Botswana, text-message problems alone had no effect. Adding a weekly 15-minute call from a person did. Plan for teachers and tutors. NBER study

High, primary-age children

Maps have failed before

Khan Academy removed its knowledge map around 2013. Duolingo replaced its skill tree with a path in 2022, because learners didn't know where to go next. A map needs one clear next step. Duolingo · Khan Academy help forum
